How It Works

From choosing your template to sharing your link — the entire process is simple, fast, and joyful. Here's exactly what happens.

01

Browse & Pick Your Template

Head over to our Templates page and explore our curated collection. Each template is handcrafted with cultural authenticity — South Indian, Telugu, North Indian, Christian, and modern styles available.

Use filters to quickly find the right style for your ceremony
Click "Demo" to see a live preview before you decide
02

Contact Us on WhatsApp

Click "Buy Now" on your chosen template to open a WhatsApp chat with us. Send us your wedding details and we'll take it from there.

Details we'll need from you:
Bride & Groom names
Wedding date & time
Venue name & address
Event schedule (Mehendi, Sangeet, etc.)
Couple photos (up to 10)
Preferred background music (optional)
Video/livestream link (optional)
Any colour preferences (optional)
03

We Design Your Invitation

Our team gets to work crafting your personalized invitation. We handle all the design, coding, and customization — you don't need any technical knowledge at all.

Day 1 — We receive your details & begin design
Day 2–3 — Build & test your invitation across all devices
Day 2–4 — Preview sent to you via WhatsApp for approval
04

Review & Approve

We send you a preview link on WhatsApp. Open it on your phone, check every detail, and let us know if anything needs to be changed. We revise until you're 100% happy.

Standard plan — revisions included until approved
Premium plan — unlimited revisions, no questions asked
05

Share & Celebrate! 🎉

Once approved, your unique link goes live. Share it anywhere — WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS, or email. Each guest receives a personalized greeting with their own name.
